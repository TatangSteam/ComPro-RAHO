### Step 2: Check SEO Files (2 minutes)

✅ Robots:  https://rahopremier.id/robots.txt

### Step 3: Google Search Console (10 minutes)
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter: `https://rahopremier.id`
4. Verify domain (choose DNS or HTML file method)
5. Click "Sitemaps" → Add: `sitemap.xml`
6. Click "Submit"

### Step 4: Test Social Sharing (5 minutes)
Share website link on:
- ✅ WhatsApp (should show logo + description)
- ✅ Instagram DM (should show preview)
- ✅ Facebook (should show rich preview)
- ✅ LinkedIn (should show professional preview)

### Step 5: Monitor (Weekly)
1. Check Google Search Console for:
   - Indexed pages (should reach 100% in 2-4 weeks)
   - Any crawl errors (fix immediately)
   - Performance data (traffic growth)
